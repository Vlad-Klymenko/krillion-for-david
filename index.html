HOW TO PLAY

Seven prompts. One little krill. A whole ocean of hidden gems.

  1. Name an answer that fits. Familiar answers earn fewer points; rare finds take you deeper.
  2. Keep trying. Wrong guesses cost nothing. Try another answer after a success; only your best answer per prompt counts.
  3. Dive again. Replay the same prompts or shuffle a new set, as often as you like.

Turn on the optional 25-second timer in Dive Settings if you want a challenge. Every retry gets a fresh clock.
